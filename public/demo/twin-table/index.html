Twin (grouped) interpolation table — reproduction

Twin interpolation (spec 016). This is a twin lookup table: Speed (rows) × Direction (columns), with an extra merged Season column on the left. Summer spans six speed rows (30–80 kts) and Winter spans four (20–60 kts) — really two stacked grids sharing the Direction axis. Enable Grid-Sight and click the S lozenge in the top-left corner to add the synced twin sliders: one shared Direction slider plus a Speed slider in each season block. The Speed sliders are synced by value; in the overlap (30–60) both blocks interpolate and highlight, and when the shared speed leaves a block's range that block's slider disables and its highlight clears. See specs/016-twin-interpolation/investigation.md.
