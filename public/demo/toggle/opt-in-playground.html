Opt-in playground

Every enrichment ships off on this page. Tick boxes in the panel on the right to enable them one at a time, then click the GS toggle on a table to inject the lozenges. Your selections persist in #gs.e=... and localStorage — clear them with the panel's reset action or open the page in a private window for a fresh start.

The Quarterly sales table below has five numeric columns and a proper header row, so it qualifies for the virtual-column enrichments: tick cumulative, diff-compare, or sparkline, click its GS toggle, then use the Σ / Δ / ⌇ lozenges.
