Statistics popup

The numeric statistics popup now reports the full column profile — missing count & %, distinct values, the quartiles (Q1 / median / Q3), and a mini histogram of the distribution — all computed over the visible rows. Apply a filter and the open popup recomputes live; a column with no numeric values shows a tidy empty state instead of an error.

Try it

  1. Click GS, then the # lozenge on the Reading column header. The popup shows the new figures and a histogram of a right-skewed distribution (a handful of large readings).
  2. Note Missing — three readings are blank. The percentage is over every cell seen, blanks included.
  3. Filter Reading (the ▽ lozenge) to a sub-range and reopen the popup: every figure recomputes over just the matching rows.
