Find in table

Click GS, then the ⌕ lozenge in the table's top-left corner to open a search box. Type a term to highlight every visible match; the counter shows your position, and Next / Previous (or Enter / Shift+Enter) step through them with wrap-around, scrolling each into view. Clearing or closing removes the highlights byte-identically.

Try it

  1. Enable GS; the ⌕ lozenge appears on the corner cell.
  2. Search a repeated term such as EU — every matching cell highlights and the counter reads e.g. 1 of 5.
  3. Step with Next/Previous (the active match gets a thicker outline, a non-colour cue); it wraps at the ends.
  4. Untick Find in table in the panel to remove the lozenge, then re-tick it — it returns without a reload.
