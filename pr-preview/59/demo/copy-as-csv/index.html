Enable Grid-Sight, then click the ⎘ lozenge in the table's top-left corner. Pick a format (CSV, TSV, or Markdown), choose what to include, and press Copy — the current visible view (after any sort and filter) lands on your clipboard, and a toast confirms the row × column count. If the clipboard is blocked, the popup offers a text box to copy from manually.

Sort or filter first to see that only the visible rows are copied. Turn the cumulative Grid-Sight column on and toggle “Grid-Sight columns” to include or omit it. The Region cell uses a rowspan to show how merged cells flatten (value at the origin, blank for spanned cells).
